A taste of the application support

Less staring at a blank page.
More knowing what to say.

The resources help you connect your real background to a specific opportunity. Here’s what that can look like.

CV TAILORING EXAMPLE

You may have more relevant experience than you think.

The role asks for
Answering customer enquiries, keeping records and escalating unresolved issues.
Your genuine experience
Helping at a community event, answering attendees’ questions and tracking registrations.
A focused way to describe it
“Answered attendee enquiries, maintained registration records and referred unresolved questions to event organisers.”

Fictional example to demonstrate the approach. Your CV must use your own facts. The prompt helps you explain relevance without inventing experience.

OUTREACH TEMPLATE EXCERPT

Give the hiring manager a reason to keep reading.

Hi [Name],

I saw your opening for [role] at [Company]. The part about [specific responsibility] stood out because [relevant connection to your actual experience].

For example, I [specific genuine work, project or contribution]. [One supported outcome or useful detail, if available.]

An excerpt from the included template. Replace the brackets with your own facts and follow the employer’s application instructions.

Before you join

Questions worth asking.

Is this the same as the free job board?

No. The public board lets you browse listings. Match uses your career profile to show personalised opportunities and includes the paid resource library and your application workspace.

What if I’m starting out or changing careers?

You can create a profile around your current skills, experience and target roles. The resources can help you explain genuine projects, coursework or volunteering. Match is not a training course, and suitable roles depend on your profile and the opportunities available.

Where will I see my matches?

Your matches appear in My Matches inside the app. Complete and save your career profile to start the matching process, then review any suitable results there. This demonstration is not a promise of the same roles, scores or number of matches for your profile.

What if there are few suitable roles for me?

Some profiles will have fewer suitable opportunities. Match does not show a role just to fill your dashboard. You can review your target roles and keep your profile accurate, use the application resources and explore the free board too. We don’t guarantee a fixed number of matches or a daily supply.

Will every role pay in dollars?

No. Some employers advertise pay in foreign currencies; others do not disclose salary or currency. Check each listing and confirm terms with the employer. Match does not guarantee a particular salary or currency.

Does remote work mean I can choose my hours?

Not necessarily. Employers set their own hours, time-zone expectations and work arrangements. Review these before applying, especially if flexibility is an important part of your decision.

Do you guarantee that I’ll get a job?

No. We help you find relevant opportunities and organise your application process. Your qualifications, the quality of your application, competition and the employer’s decision all affect the outcome.

How many matches will I receive?

There is no fixed number. Available matches depend on your profile and the verified roles currently available. Suitable opportunities change as employers open and close vacancies.

Do the prompts automatically rewrite my documents inside Match?

No. Copy the prompt, your CV and the relevant job description into your preferred AI tool. Review its output before applying. Any charges from your chosen AI tool are separate from Match access.

What happens when my 12 weeks end?

Matching and resource-library access end unless you renew. Your application tracker remains accessible so you can keep track of applications already in progress. The separately listed renewal offer is ₦15,000 for another 12 weeks; renewal is optional.

What is your refund policy?

Once paid matching or resource content has been used, we do not offer change-of-mind refunds. Duplicate charges and access problems are reviewed for correction or refund where appropriate. Read the full refund policy.

Can I download the resource ebooks?

Guides are provided for in-app reading during your access period. Prompts and outreach templates can be copied and adapted for your own applications.

Is the free CV optimiser included in this payment?

The CV optimiser is a separate free beta. Match’s resource library includes CV-tailoring and cover-letter prompts for individual jobs. You can try the free CV beta here.
